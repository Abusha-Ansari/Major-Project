📋 How to Participate

  1. Register your hospital above to get a unique token.
  2. Open the Hospital Desktop App on your machine.
  3. Enter your hospital name and Login to retrieve the token.
  4. Point the app to your local X-ray dataset folder.
  5. Click Train & Submit — the app handles everything.
  6. Wait for the admin to aggregate all hospital updates.
  7. Repeat from step 3 for the next federated round.

Privacy note: Only model weight updates are shared — never your raw patient images. The server cannot reconstruct your data from the weight deltas.
